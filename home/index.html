Your Cyprus

Nine thousand years, one island

Kypros is a map of Cyprus you can read five ways: nine thousand years of history, the wine villages of the hills, the island's wild places, its stone and craft villages, and the foods each village is known for. Pick a view, open the places on it, and plan days out between them. When you visit, check in and watch the island fill in.

Getting around the map

Five things to try first

  1. Switch the view

    History, Wine, Nature, Villages and Table sit at the top of the panel. The map changes its look, its places and its key.

  2. Open a place

    Tap a pin or a name in the list for its story, photos, how long to allow and the drive to nearby places.

  3. Step through time

    On the historical map, move period by period from the first villages to the Ottoman island.

  4. Plan a day

    Choose Plan a day for ready-made trips, or pick your interests and let the map fit the best stops into your time.

  5. Tilt into 3D

    The mountain button raises the terrain, so you can see the Troodos and the Kyrenia range rise from the plain.

Five maps, one island

Five ways to see Cyprus

The same island, read five ways. Switch between them on the map at any time; every place belongs to one view, and many sit only a short drive from places in the others.
